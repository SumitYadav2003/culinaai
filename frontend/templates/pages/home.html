How it works

Three steps from a half-full fridge to dinner.

  1. Add your ingredients

    Type what you have, pick from your pantry list, or take a photo of your fridge and let CulinaAI read it.

  2. Say what you need

    Choose your diet, allergies, cooking time, budget and cuisine, and whether you want an everyday meal or a treat.

  3. Cook it

    Follow one step at a time in cooking mode, with timers and voice control. Save the recipes you like for next time.

What comes with every recipe

The AI writes the recipe. The checks and numbers are worked out separately, from published food data.

Checked for your allergies and diet

Before you see a recipe, it's checked against the allergies and diet you entered. If something doesn't fit, it's written again.

Nutrition you can trust

Calories, fat, sugar and salt per serving, from the UK government's food tables, with the same traffic lights as food labels.

Cost and carbon

A rough cost per serving from UK supermarket prices, and the carbon footprint of the ingredients.

Cheaper, healthier or greener

See the same dish made cheaper, healthier or lower in carbon, and cook whichever version suits you.

Hands-free cooking mode

Say "next", "repeat" or "set a timer for five minutes" without touching your phone with messy hands.

Learns how you cook

Tell it how each step went. Next time, timings and tips are adjusted to your pace.
