Our Mission

To make cooking more accessible, personalised and efficient.

Safety & Responsibility

Designed to support cooking decisions, not replace user judgement.

CulinaAI can provide allergy and dietary guidance, but users should always manually check ingredients, labels and medical or dietary requirements before cooking or consuming any recipe.
