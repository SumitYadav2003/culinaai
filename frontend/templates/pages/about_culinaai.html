About CulinaAI

CulinaAI turns the food you already have into a recipe that fits your diet and allergies. Then it works out the nutrition, cost and carbon, so you can see what you're cooking before you start.

What it does

  • Makes a recipe from what you have. Type your ingredients, use your pantry list, or take a photo of your fridge.
  • Checks it against your allergies and diet. If a recipe doesn't fit, it's written again before you see it.
  • Shows what's in it. Calories, fat, sugar and salt per serving with UK traffic-light levels, plus a rough cost and carbon footprint.
  • Offers cheaper, healthier and greener versions. The same dish with a few ingredients swapped, and the difference worked out.
  • Talks you through cooking. One step at a time, with timers and voice control, and timings that adjust to how you cook.
  • Keeps your recipes. Save, change, print, email or share them with other cooks.

How recipes are checked

An AI model writes each recipe. Separate code then checks it: that it leaves out what you said you can't eat, fits your diet, uses your ingredients, keeps to your cooking time and only needs the equipment you have. A recipe that fails a safety check is written again.

The numbers are not written by the AI. Nutrition comes from the UK's food composition data (CoFID), cost from UK supermarket and ONS prices, and carbon from published averages per kilo of food. More about the numbers and what they add up to.

What it can't do

  • It can't see the labels on what you buy. Always check them yourself, especially for allergens.
  • The nutrition, cost and carbon figures are estimates, not medical or dietary advice. If you have a medical condition or follow a clinical diet, speak to your GP or a registered dietitian.
  • Recipe pictures are made by AI to show the idea of the dish. The real thing will look different.
