Four UN goals, one kitchen

Everyday cooking touches four of the UN Sustainable Development Goals. Each starts with something you can do tonight.

  • 1

    No Poverty

    Spend less on good food. Supports food affordability: every recipe shows its cost per serving, and the Cheapest version shows what each swap saves.

  • 2

    Zero Hunger

    Turn what you have into a proper meal. Recipes start from your own ingredients, your pantry or a photo of your fridge.

  • 3

    Good Health and Well-being

    Know what's on your plate. UK traffic lights, allergen warnings and a Healthiest version, all worked out by code.

  • 12

    Responsible Consumption

    Waste less, emit less. See each dish's carbon, cook the Greenest version, and use food before it goes out of date.

Sources

The fine print: where the numbers come from, and what they can't do
  • Nutrition: UK CoFID 2021 food composition data, with a few USDA foods, each labelled. Raw ingredient weights, so cooking changes the real figures a little.
  • Health levels: UK front-of-pack traffic lights and UK nutrition claim conditions. Not medical or dietary advice.
  • Cost: ONS average prices (August 2026) and Tesco, Sainsbury's and Morrisons prices (October 2026). Prices vary by shop and week.
  • Carbon: global averages per kg of food from Poore & Nemecek (2018), via Our World in Data. Beef uses the beef-herd figure; UK beef from dairy herds is about a third of that, so beef savings may read high.
  • The example: worked out by the same code as every recipe page. The yearly figure multiplies one serving's saving by the servings in a year.
  • Your figures: from the recipes you made, not the meals you ate.
  • The goals: CulinaAI gives people information and choices that relate to UN Sustainable Development Goals 1, 2, 3 and 12 (including targets 2.1, 12.3 and 12.8). It does not measure progress towards them.
