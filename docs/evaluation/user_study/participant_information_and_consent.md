## What is this study about?

CulinaAI is a website that makes recipes from the ingredients you have, checks them
for allergies, diet and nutrition, and talks you through cooking with a voice
assistant. I'm testing how easy it is to use and where it goes wrong.

## What will I be asked to do?

- Make a recipe with CulinaAI using ingredients you choose.
- Follow it in cooking mode, using the voice assistant. You can cook the dish, or just
  go through the steps without cooking.
- Fill in a short questionnaire and chat about what you thought.

It takes about 45 to 60 minutes if you cook, or about 25 minutes if you don't.

## Do I have to take part?

No. You can stop at any time without giving a reason. You can also ask for your
answers to be removed up until (add a date, for example when the analysis starts).

## Are there any risks?

Cooking carries the usual kitchen risks of heat and knives. Please cook only as you
normally would at home. CulinaAI's allergy checks are not a guarantee: if you have a
food allergy, please don't eat anything made in this study unless you have checked
every ingredient yourself, as you always would.

## What happens to my information?

- You'll be given a code (P1, P2, ...). Your name is only on this consent form, which
  is kept separately from your answers.
- Your answers, my notes and the test account's recipes are used only for this study.
- Results are reported as totals and averages. I may quote what you said, under your
  code, never your name.
- I won't record audio or video. The voice assistant uses your browser's speech
  recognition: in Chrome and Edge your speech is turned into text by the browser
  maker's online speech service (Google or Microsoft), and CulinaAI only receives the
  text. CulinaAI doesn't keep recordings. What you say or type when it asks how a step
  went is saved as a short text note in the test account.
- Your data will be kept until (add the date the project ends) and then deleted.
