# Voice checklist

Participant code: ______  Device and browser: ____________________

Ask the participant to try each command at a natural moment while cooking, in their
own words. The examples are only there if they can't think of a way to say it. Tick
one box per command.

- **First time:** CulinaAI did the right thing straight away.
- **After repeating:** it worked on the second or third try.
- **Not understood:** it did the wrong thing or nothing, or they gave up.

Write down what they actually said when it didn't work first time; those sentences
are the most useful thing to come out of this sheet.
